CS 250  -  Lab 15: PyPaws Pet Shelter
=====================================

Open  Lab15_Guide.pdf  and follow it step by step.

Quick start:
  1. Open this WHOLE folder (Lab15_PyPaws) in your IDE.
  2. Run  check_my_work.py  and press Enter. Lots of FAIL is normal - you have not started yet!
  3. Start with Step 1 in pet.py.

Files you will edit (in this order):
  pet.py          Step 1 + Step 2   (classes, attributes, methods)
  shelter.py      Step 3 + Step 5   (files + exceptions)
  bug_hunt.py     Step 4            (debugging)
  bug_report.txt  Step 4            (write what you found)
  main.py         Step 5            (input validation, then PLAY!)

Do NOT edit:
  check_my_work.py   (your self-checker)
  data/pets.csv      (the broken lines are there on purpose!)

How to submit (details on the "Submit your work" page of the guide):
  1. Run check_my_work.py, screenshot the score, save it in this folder as score.png
  2. Upload this whole Lab15_PyPaws folder to your CS 250 labs repository on GitHub
  3. Paste the GitHub link to the Lab15_PyPaws folder in the Lab 15 assignment on Canvas
